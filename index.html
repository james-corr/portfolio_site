Output

Multi-user SaaS. Live.

getoutput.app
Problem
Teammates were manually copying meeting action items out of a notes tool into the team's task board after every call — and softer signals, like customer sentiment and renewal risk, never got captured anywhere at all.
Decision
Built a human-in-the-loop Slack approve or deny flow that captures both action items and sentiment signals straight out of the meeting, scored by Claude Haiku, instead of writing either straight into the board or the CRM, so nothing lands without a person confirming it. Onboarding runs through a six-step wizard on a custom design system.
Outcome
Live multi-user SaaS in daily use by teammates: confirmed action items write to the task board, confirmed sentiment writes to the CRM, handling OAuth across three services with encrypted credentials, crash-safe polling, and automatic retries end to end.
Stack
Node.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Claude Haiku, Fly.io

Postgame

CS command center. Built during my time at Freshpaint, still running.

Problem
Renewal-risk signal was scattered — sentiment sitting in the CRM, call recordings and scores sitting in call intelligence tools like Gong — so leadership had to manually stitch together account health before every forecast.
Decision
Built an aggregation and visualization layer instead of another point tool: it pulls signal from wherever it already lives, Output's sentiment feed inside the CRM, call intelligence platforms like Gong, and feeds it to Claude Sonnet, which evaluates sentiment across the whole book and writes the daily narrative.
Outcome
A daily leadership briefing scoring renewal risk across every account inside its 90-day window, combined Gong-plus-human sentiment, and the specific accounts that need attention that week, built from signal that used to live in three different places.
Stack
Node.js, TypeScript, Postgres, Claude Sonnet, Fly.io

ClienTell

Research automation. Built during my time at Freshpaint, still running.

clientell.fly.dev
Problem
Pre-call research meant manually digging through a company's website to figure out who they already work with.
Decision
Built a crawler with three depth settings (homepage, 20-page scan, 50-page scan) that reads client mentions with confidence scoring and industry inference, instead of a flat page-by-page manual search.
Outcome
Saved the team roughly 5 hours a month previously spent on manual research and prospecting, with results exportable straight to CSV.
Stack
React, Express, Postgres, Playwright, Claude API

Daily Brief Agent

Enterprise agent. Built during my time at Freshpaint, still running.

Problem
Everyone started the day cold, with no context on the accounts and partners already sitting on the calendar.
Decision
Built on an enterprise agent platform rather than a one-off script, pulling from calendar, knowledge base, and CRM sources so the brief stays current as those systems change.
Outcome
A personalized morning brief landed for every user on the team before their first meeting, cutting manual pre-meeting prep.
