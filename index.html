Output

Multi-user SaaS. Live.

getoutput.app
Problem
Teammates were manually copying meeting action items out of a notes tool into the team's task board after every call, a small task that quietly ate real time every day.
Decision
Built a human-in-the-loop Slack approve or deny flow instead of writing tasks straight into the board, so nothing lands without a person confirming it. Onboarding runs through a six-step wizard on a custom design system.
Outcome
Live multi-user SaaS that went into daily use by teammates, handling OAuth across three services with encrypted credentials, crash-safe polling, and automatic retries end to end.
Stack
Node.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Claude API, Fly.io

Postgame

CS command center. Built at Freshpaint, still running.

Problem
CSMs were surfacing renewal risk on customer calls, then losing it the moment the call ended.
Decision
Human in the loop by design: the model drafts the read on sentiment and renewal confidence, the CSM confirms it in a Slack modal, so nothing fabricated reaches the CRM.
Outcome
Every confirmed answer writes back onto the call's CRM record and rolls up into a leadership readout scoring renewal risk across every account inside its 90-day window.
Stack
Node.js, TypeScript, Postgres, Claude Haiku, Fly.io

ClienTell

Research automation. Live.

clientell.fly.dev
Problem
Pre-call research meant manually digging through a company's website to figure out who they already work with.
Decision
Built a crawler with three depth settings (homepage, 20-page scan, 50-page scan) that reads client mentions with confidence scoring and industry inference, instead of a flat page-by-page manual search.
Outcome
Saved the team roughly 5 hours a month previously spent on manual research and prospecting, with results exportable straight to CSV.
Stack
React, Express, Postgres, Playwright, Claude API

Daily Brief Agent

Enterprise agent. Built at Freshpaint, still running.

Problem
Everyone started the day cold, with no context on the accounts and partners already sitting on the calendar.
Decision
Built on an enterprise agent platform rather than a one-off script, pulling from calendar, knowledge base, and CRM sources so the brief stays current as those systems change.
Outcome
A personalized morning brief landed for every user on the team before their first meeting, cutting manual pre-meeting prep.
Stack
Glean, Google Calendar, Notion, HubSpot

Focus Quest

Productivity tool.

Problem
Most Pomodoro timers either nag constantly or get abandoned after a week.
Decision
Built points, streaks, and badges on top of the timer instead of a bare countdown, with wall-clock accurate timing that survives the tab going to sleep.
Outcome
A calm, distraction-free daily-use tool with session history to track focus habits over time.
Stack
Next.js 15, TypeScript, Supabase, Docker, Fly.io
